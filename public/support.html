Letterlings

Help for grown-ups

Letterlings teaches children aged 5 to 10 to type on a real keyboard, starting with the home row and working up to capitals, numbers, symbols and accents across seven worlds.

Does it need a keyboard?

Yes. It's made for real typing, not touch screens. On an iPad, connect a Bluetooth or Smart Keyboard first. On a Mac, the built-in keyboard is perfect.

How long should my child play?

Short, regular practice works best: about 10 minutes a day for younger children, or 15–20 minutes a few times a week. "Today's Adventure" on the map is a good-sized session. You can also set a daily time limit in the Parent Zone.

The Parent Zone

On the "Who's playing?" screen, press Grown-ups and answer the times-table question. There you can:

  • see a progress report for each child, including which keys to practise next;
  • start a player over, or delete a player and all their progress;
  • change sound, music, reading aloud, motion and text settings;
  • set a daily play limit;
  • save or load a backup file to move progress to another device.

The letters on the on-screen keyboard disappear. Is that a bug?

No. As your child masters a key, its letter fades from the on-screen keyboard so they learn to find it by feel. Ages 5–6 always keep every letter. To keep them on for everyone, turn on "Always show key letters" in the Parent Zone.
