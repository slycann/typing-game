Letterlings

Privacy Policy

Effective November 3, 2026

Letterlings is a typing game for children aged 5 to 10, made by Sylvester Cann. It is built so that it does not collect any personal information. This policy covers the Letterlings app for iPad and Mac and the website at slycann.github.io/typing-game.

The short version

We collect nothing. No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking, and no third-party code that collects data.

What stays on your device

  • Each player's first name, chosen character and age group (5–6, 7–8 or 9–10).
  • Game progress: stars, coins, shop items, stickers, typing speed and which keys need practice.
  • Settings such as volume and the daily play limit, and initials typed on the device's high-score board.

All of this is stored only on the device the game is played on. It is never sent to us or to anyone else. A grown-up can view, reset or delete any player at any time in the game's Parent Zone (the "Grown-ups" button on the player screen).

Backups

A grown-up can choose to save a backup file from the Parent Zone. The file is saved only where they choose, and is never uploaded by the game.

Speech

Characters talk using the voice built into your device. The words are spoken on the device itself; nothing is sent over the internet.

Children's privacy

Letterlings is made for children. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, there is nothing to share, sell or disclose. There are no in-app purchases, no ads, and no links that leave the game.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
